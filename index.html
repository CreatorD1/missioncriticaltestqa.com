06 / FAQ

Questions before we begin.

Straight answers about the engagement, the tools, and what your team owns when the cycle ends.

Why use a 21-day cycle instead of hiring full-time?

You get senior QA, performance, and reliability coverage for a defined release risk without adding permanent headcount. The cycle is scoped around an outcome, not an open-ended seat on the team.

Which tools will you use?

The stack is chosen around your application and team. The default is free and open-source: Cypress or Playwright for browser automation, k6 for load, and Grafana or Uptime Kuma for monitoring.

What happens after the 21 days?

You receive the working test assets, performance findings, monitoring setup, handover documentation, and a walkthrough. Your team can continue independently, book targeted hourly help, or scope another feature cycle.

How does remote delivery stay accountable?

You set the goal and acceptance criteria. We deliver autonomously against a visible schedule, report evidence honestly, and surface bad news as clearly as good news—without vanity metrics or daily hand-holding.
